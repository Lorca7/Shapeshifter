How the shape survives export

A raster file is always a rectangle of pixels. Shapeshifter crops that rectangle tight to your outline and writes every pixel outside the outline as fully transparent, so PNG and WebP open as the shape you drew. SVG also carries the outline as a real vector clip path, so it stays sharp at any size. JPEG has no transparency at all, which is why choosing it fills the outside with a solid colour instead.
