**Shapeshifter for Mac.** Trace an object in a photo, erase everything else, and save it in its own shape.

Runs on macOS 13 or newer, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

## Install

1. Download **Shapeshifter.dmg** below and double-click it.
2. Drag **Shapeshifter** onto the **Applications** folder in the window that opens.
3. Eject the disk image (drag it to the Trash, or click ⏏ next to it in Finder).

## The first time you open it

Because this app isn't registered with Apple, macOS asks you to confirm it once:

1. Open your **Applications** folder.
2. **Right-click** (or Control-click) **Shapeshifter** and choose **Open**.
3. Click **Open** in the dialog that appears.

After that it opens normally. If you double-clicked it first and got a "cannot be opened"
message, open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click
**Open Anyway** next to Shapeshifter.

## Using it

1. Drop a photo on the window (JPEG, PNG or TIFF).
2. Click along the edge of the object to drop points. Zoom in for precision.
3. Click the first point to close the outline. Everything outside it is erased.
4. Drag any point to fine-tune it, or click an edge to add one.
5. **Save cutout.** The PNG opens as the shape you traced, not as a rectangle.

Nothing is uploaded. The app makes no network connections at all.
